## 1. Design: choosing the proof-of-purchase model

- **Phase:** Planning
- **Prompt:** Asked the AI to plan a loyalty system (proof of purchase, fraud prevention, owner dashboard).
- **What the AI did:** Proposed generated unique codes printed on boxes, with batch generation and QR export.
- **Was it wrong?** Not wrong, but I redirected it.
- **How I noticed:** The client already holds product and invoice data, so generating and printing codes was unnecessary work.
- **Fix / decision:** Switched to company-supplied coupon codes stored on the product row, matched with an invoice number.
